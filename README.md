Entry for the make.fun bounty [One Line to Your Younger Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-boothlord-one-line-to-your-younger-self-22e97f76).

The program writes a single line — the one line its writer would send back
to their younger self if one line were all they got — to `/output/entry.txt`.

> When mum says the computer will ruin your eyes, thank her — then buy her the glasses she never bought herself with your first paycheck.

Run it locally with `ENTRY_OUTPUT=/tmp/entry.txt python3 main.py`.
